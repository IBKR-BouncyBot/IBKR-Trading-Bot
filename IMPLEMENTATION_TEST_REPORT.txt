BouncyBot - IBKR Portable Trading Bot v3.6.0
SELL reconciliation and Trade History robustness report
Release date: 2026-07-29

LINEAGE AND REVIEW
==================

This release is based on the final v3.5.0 source archive. The submitted local
v3.5.0 source and patch were compared with that baseline, reviewed, integrated,
and advanced to v3.6.0.

The submitted changes added fail-closed final/protective SELL quantity gates,
numeric sorting for formatted Trade History columns, and operator-visible GUI
errors for audit reads and history exports. Review identified and corrected
related edge cases around cancel-and-replace exit aggregation, a still-working
partially filled SELL, missing persisted execution proof, missing aggregate fill
price, and non-finite numeric sort values.

IMPLEMENTED CHANGES
===================

1. Exact aggregate final-SELL settlement

- A cycle completes only when persisted cycle SELL executions exactly equal the
  app-owned BUY-filled quantity, no broker remainder is still working, and an
  aggregate fill price is available.
- Nonterminal partial fills are persisted and remain under supervision.
- Terminal partials enter Stage.ERROR with SELL_PARTIAL_TERMINAL.
- Underfills, overfills, contradictory remainders, missing execution proof, and
  missing aggregate fill prices enter Stage.ERROR with SELL_QUANTITY_MISMATCH.
- Cancel-and-replace and manual-close remainder orders are validated against all
  app-owned SELL executions for the cycle. Weighted aggregate price, commission,
  and final P/L therefore include both the earlier partial and replacement fill.
- A partially filled order remains working until its broker status is terminal,
  so manual market close cancels and confirms the existing remainder before a
  replacement can be submitted.

2. Protective-SELL terminal consistency

- A terminal protective SELL that reports the full app-owned filled quantity and
  a positive remainder is treated as an unprovable quantity mismatch. It cannot
  remain in an endless polling state or be interpreted as exact closure.
- The existing v3.4.0 exact-quantity, terminal-partial, recovery, and fail-closed
  protective-SELL gates remain in force.

3. Trade History and audit GUI robustness

- Formatted currency, percentage, and quantity cells sort by their raw finite
  numeric values instead of lexical display text.
- Blank, invalid, NaN, and infinite values group deterministically below finite
  values.
- Audit-detail read failures and Trade History export failures are shown in an
  operator dialog. A failed export does not display a success message, and a
  failed audit read does not open a partially initialized dialog.

SAFETY AND COMPATIBILITY
========================

- Ambiguous SELL facts fail closed for manual reconciliation instead of
  completing a cycle without exact broker/local proof.
- Entry logic, ATR calculations, strategy percentages, BUY construction,
  position sizing, normal SELL order construction, market-data selection, quote
  freshness, RTH/session behavior, complete-OrderRef ownership, watchdog
  restart, and account/contract scope are unchanged.
- v3.6.0 adds no SQLite table, column, index, migration, or persisted setting.
- Existing v3.5.0 databases, active cycles, execution rows, checkpoints,
  watchdog files, captures, backups, and portable folders remain compatible.
